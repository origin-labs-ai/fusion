\chapter{The codec}

\section{The block codec}

\texttt{block\_codec.cpp} is the codec heart: the per-family encoders, the shared scale-fitter,
the budget guards. The known traps (D-20): \texttt{test\_block\_codec.cpp} actually tests GEMM
kernels (the mis-named test), and the fuzz allowance permits claimed+1 byte (S3 kills the
allowance). The budget-guard pattern of \texttt{quant\_grp16:879-884} is the real budget test ---
never the \texttt{has\_d = (n >= 8)} non-test that caused the quant\_6k overrun.

\section{The scale fitter}

The shared scale-fitter \texttt{fit\_scale\_true\_mse} (block\_codec.cpp:632--695): LS seed
$\to$ 9-point grid $\to$ 12-iteration golden-section refinement $\to$ FP16 snap $\pm6$
neighborhood sweep. Golden-section on a unimodal MSE curve shrinks the bracket by
$\varphi \approx 0.618$ per iteration; twelve iterations leave $0.618^{12} \approx 0.0045$ of
the original bracket --- enough to resolve the scale to FP16 resolution. The FP16 snap sweep
repairs the quantized-scale error after the continuous fit. The old $Q_{12}$ 3-iteration LS
seed measured 46~dB while K\_H's 24 iterations reach ${\sim}59$~dB; the acceptance gate closes
the gap.

The FormatPlanner analyzes a model with calibration data and allocates formats per block to
hit a target BPW. The STE quantizer makes codebooks trainable with the straight-through
estimator; QAT trains in-format; the native trainer keeps its separate id-space documented
(D-20).

\section{Import honesty}

The GGUF bridge silently zero-filled unsupported types (\texttt{gguf\_bridge.cpp:336--342},
verbose-gated) --- silent zero weights are silent corruption. The L5 pass raises the fill to a
loud fail, documented as part of import honesty. The import path streams HuggingFace
checkpoints through \texttt{hf\_streamer.h} and \texttt{parquet\_streamer.h} with
\texttt{mmap\_dataloader.h}; the qwen35 engine and tokenizer adapters carry the import-side
engine glue.

\section{The GPU capability contract}

\texttt{test\_gpu\_capability} asserts the fail-loud contract: a backend that cannot deliver
reports loud, never silent fallback. The KAT inventory names the 5th attention implementation
in \texttt{src/backend/}: 4 fused + 1 composed + 15 primitive-only. The roughly eighteen GPU
backends that exist but are unavailable are the documented exception to zero-dead-code (D-07):
kept, accurate, excluded from the rule until the backend-completion effort.
