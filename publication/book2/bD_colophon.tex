\section*{How this book was made}

This book is the hand-authored edition of the 2026-11-14 round. The narrative chapters were
written against the project's knowledge base: LADDER.md, DECISIONS.md, THEOREM\_CENDER.md, the
live enum, BRAND.md, windows.json and the live-verified arXiv abstracts. The appendices are
computed from the live tree by pure-Python generators: the format table parses LADDER.md's
keep-list and computes every format's wire arithmetic from its BPW; the source-file cards read
every engine file and every skill directory; the ledgers count and hash the documents.
