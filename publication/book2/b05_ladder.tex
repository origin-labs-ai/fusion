\chapter{The ladder}

The 69-format contract (LADDER.md is the source). This chapter carries the rules, the rewire
map and the retire list; the deep-dive arithmetic is computed in the appendices.

\section{Rules the ladder encodes}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Family pattern:} $B$ = base at exact $.00$ --- the L tier. M = $B+0.25$,
H = $B+0.50$ --- three genuinely different codecs (the old L$\equiv$M$\equiv$H lie ends).
\item \textbf{$Q_1$ family:} L = $Q_1$ = binary slot-sign, exact 1.00; M = 1.25;
H = $Q_{1\_K\_H}$ = pure ternary, exact 1.50.
\item \textbf{GRP base rewire down to name:} $2.625\to2.00$, $3.5\to3.0$, $4.5\to4.0$,
$6.5625\to6.0$, $8.5\to8.0$, $12.5\to12.0$, $\to16.0$, $\to24.0$.
\item \textbf{K\_G M/H take the M/H tiers;} K\_L ids retire (plain and GRP).
\item \textbf{Half formats fold:} the 18 half ids (73--90) retire; their wire spaces land in
the \texttt{\_K\_H} tiers at the same BPW; negative-gap names retire without rewire.
\item \textbf{MX quarantine:} all 14 MX ids keep, quarantine-marked;
$Q_{G\_MX\_3\_5}$ rewires $3.78125\to3.50$.
\item \textbf{$Q_{32}$ float ceiling:} no tiers --- lossless is lossless.
\item \textbf{ID strategy:} keep surviving ids in place, retire the rest, no renumber (D-08).
\end{enumerate}

\section{The tier arithmetic}

M tier: $+1$ refinement byte per 32 weights $= 8$ bytes per 256-weight block
$= +0.25$ BPW exactly. H tier: $+16$ bytes per block $= +0.50$ BPW exactly.
GRP M: $+8$~B of per-16 4-bit group scale codes. GRP H: $+16$~B of per-16 affine state.
